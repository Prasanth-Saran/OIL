OIL FIELD SAFETY REPORT
Report ID: OIL-HSE-2026-0352
Date: 2026-04-19
Location: Crude Storage Terminal - Tank TK-104, Duliajan
Report Type: Near Miss
Reported By: Terminal Safety Auditor

NARRATIVE / DESCRIPTION:
During pre-cleaning inspection of crude oil storage tank TK-104 (diameter 32m, height 14m), a contract cleaning foreman and two laborers were found inside the tank manway opening preparing sludge removal shovels. 

The safety audit team reviewed the Permit to Work (PTW) documentation on-site and verified that:
1) The Confined Space Entry permit had not been signed off by the Authorized Gas Tester (AGT).
2) Forced draft ventilation blowers had not been powered on.
3) Oxygen level check was not recorded (last reading was 18.2% two days prior).
4) No dedicated standby attendant was stationed at the manway with a retrieval winch.

The workers were immediately ordered out of the vessel. Entry into an uncertified confined space with potential hydrocarbon residue and oxygen deficiency represents a severe potential SIF precursor event.
