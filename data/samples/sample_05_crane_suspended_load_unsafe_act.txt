OIL FIELD SAFETY REPORT
Report ID: OIL-HSE-2026-0351
Date: 2026-04-18
Location: Pipe Yard & Rigging Base, Moran Field
Report Type: Unsafe Act
Reported By: Yard Logistics Supervisor

NARRATIVE / DESCRIPTION:
During heavy cargo offloading operations, a 30-ton mobile hydraulic crane was observed slewing a bundled lift of three 8-inch drill collars (total load weight approx. 4.2 metric tons) across the central access road. 

Two roustabouts were walking directly underneath the suspended path of the boom without guiding taglines attached to the load. The crane banksman was distracted and failed to sound the horn or barricade the pedestrian corridor. 

The lifting supervisor halted the lift immediately. Inspection of the lifting gear showed the web slings were properly rated and intact, but personnel positioning under suspended loads constitutes a serious unsafe act in violation of corporate lifting safety standards.
