OIL FIELD SAFETY REPORT
Report ID: OIL-HSE-2026-0348
Date: 2026-04-14
Location: Drilling Rig #09 - Mast & Derrick, Moran Field
Report Type: Unsafe Act
Reported By: Toolpusher / Assistant Drilling Superintendent

NARRATIVE / DESCRIPTION:
During tripping-in operations of 5-inch drill pipe, the derrickman was observed working on the monkey board platform at an elevation of approximately 24 meters above the drill floor. 

The safety inspection revealed that although the worker was wearing a full-body safety harness, the inertia reel fall-arrest lanyard was unhooked and draped over the handrail rather than anchored to the certified overhead lifeline cable. Furthermore, the worker was leaning out over the racking fingers to catch a swaying drill pipe stand without any secondary tie-off.

Work was immediately suspended via Stop Work Authority (SWA). The derrickman stated that he had detached the lanyard temporarily to reach a stuck pipe latch. The potential consequence of a fall from this elevation without positive arrest is a fatal fall event (SIF precursor).
